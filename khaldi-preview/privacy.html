Privacy information

This page explains how the contact options in this version of our website work.

Contact and quotations

When you select “Open email app to send” on our quotation page, your email application opens with a draft message. The website does not directly submit that form or store its contents on a server. You decide whether to send the email.

Communication

If you email Khaldi Group, your message and the contact details you provide can be used to respond to your inquiry. For questions about the handling of your correspondence, contact info@khaldi-group.com.

Browser preferences

Your chosen light or dark appearance may be saved locally in your browser so the setting can be remembered on your next visit.

Additional services

If analytics, server-side quotation submissions, or other services are added, this information should be reviewed and updated to match how those services process data.
